About

Turning Atmosphere
Into Quantitative Insight

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Houston, where I received my PhD in Atmospheric Science. My research sits at the intersection of large-scale climate model diagnostics, observational data engineering, and applied machine learning — with a focus on producing validated, reproducible, decision-ready signals from raw atmospheric data.

I work with iCAM6, CESM, ERA5, and a full suite of multi-observational precipitation products (MRMS, IMERG, CPC, GPCC, GPCP, CRU) on NCAR's Derecho HPC system. My Python workflows process tens of terabytes of NetCDF/GRIB output into bias-corrected, area-weighted regional metrics.

Beyond climate model work, my PhD research applied Computational Fluid Dynamics (OpenFOAM LES/DNS) and deep learning to atmospheric dispersion, boundary-layer turbulence, air quality forecasting, and convective dynamics. I have published across Physics of Fluids, Scientific Reports, Earth Systems and Environment, and Science of the Total Environment.

Moisture Sources of Southwest North American Precipitation: Four Tracking Frameworks

Where does the rain over the US Southwest and northern Mexico come from? I trace it to local land, the North Pacific and the Gulf of Mexico with four different methods: online water tagging in WRF and in iCAM6, and offline one- and two-layer dynamic recycling models driven by ERA5, all with the same sources, targets and 36 years. The frameworks agree on the seasonal cycle of source contributions but differ in magnitudes and year-to-year variability, so any single framework's attribution is best read against the spread among them.

Frameworks WRF-WVT · iCAM6 tags · 1L / 2L-DRM
Period 1979–2014 (36 years)
Sources Local land · N. Pacific · Gulf of Mexico
Systems NCAR Derecho · UH Carya

Manuscript in preparation · 2L-DRM ~150× cheaper than three WRF-WVT runs · code: WRF-WVT-multitag (extends WRF-WVTs by Insua-Costa & Miguez-Macho), era5-to-wps

WRF-Chem OpenFOAM LES LiDAR

Multi-Scale Urban Air Quality: WRF-Chem Coupled to OpenFOAM LES

A downscaling chain from regional chemistry to the street: WRF-Chem meteorology and chemistry drive a building-resolving large-eddy simulation of a Houston neighborhood. Buildings and vegetation come from LiDAR, freeway and biogenic emissions are injected on the urban geometry, and a reacting OpenFOAM solver with SAPRC photochemistry resolves NO₂ and O₃ between buildings through a full diurnal cycle.

Domain 600 × 600 × 100 m, Houston
Resolution ~1.25 m near the surface
Chemistry SAPRC-07 (CS07A) · reacting OpenFOAM
Validation Tracer field & wind-tunnel data

Manuscript with Y. Choi · LiDAR-to-mesh pipeline · runs on UH Carya HPC

CESM SMYLE Ensemble El Niño IOA · Corr

SMYLE 2015/16 El Niño: Seasonal Forecast Verification

Why did the record 2015/16 El Niño leave California dry? I verify the CESM SMYLE ensemble's winter forecast member by member against gauge, radar, satellite and reanalysis precipitation, using the spread among those observations as the uncertainty bar. Ranking members by precipitation skill shows that the best precipitation member is not the one with the best moisture and circulation.

Season DJF 2015/16
Observations ERA5 · MRMS · CPC · GPCC · GPCP · IMERG
Metrics Willmott IOA · Corr · Bias · RMSE
Variables Precip · Z200/500/750 · PSL · Q850 · TMQ

Key finding: precipitation-best member ≠ moisture-best member

OpenFOAM DNS · LES Convection ML

Turbulent Convection in the Pi Cloud Chamber

Rayleigh–Bénard dry convection in the 2 m × 2 m Pi cloud chamber, simulated with OpenFOAM DNS and LES. Roughness bars on the floor (different heights, layouts and orientations) reorganize the large-scale circulation and the near-wall heat transfer. DNS then serves as the reference for wall-modeled LES whose wall fluxes are improved with machine learning.

Methods DNS · LES · wall-modeled LES
Cases Smooth vs rough floors

WRF Fortran Moisture Tracers MIT

WRF-WVT multi-tag

Runs WRF with up to 10 moisture tags at once, each with its own source region, instead of one tag per run. Built on WRF-WVTs (Insua-Costa & Miguez-Macho) as a patch for WRF 4.3.3; in the 2- and 3-tag tests, every tag is bit-for-bit identical to its single-tag run.

Tags per run 1–10 (more after a rebuild)
Cost 3 tags ≈ 1.6 single-tag runs
Physics WSM6 · Kain–Fritsch · YSU
Validated 2 and 3 tags vs WRF-WVTs

github.com/hadizkia/WRF-WVT-multitag

ERA5 WPS Python MIT

era5-to-wps

Converts ERA5 from NCAR's NetCDF archive (RDA ds633.0) straight into WPS intermediate files for metgrid, with no GRIB files or ungrib. Writes only a window around the WRF domain and reproduces ungrib's derived fields.

Input ERA5 0.25° NetCDF (RDA)
Output WPS intermediate, v5
Stack Python · numpy · xarray
Used for ERA5-driven, nudged WRF runs

github.com/hadizkia/era5-to-wps
